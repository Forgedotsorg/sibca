Who We Are

Nearly five decades of engineering-led building technology.

Established in Abu Dhabi in 1977, SIBCA (SIBCA Electronics Equipment Co. / SIBCA Group) began with SMATV and Extra-Low Voltage work, and has grown into a multi-discipline systems integration group spanning Fire & Life Safety, Building Management Systems, Security, HVAC, Audio-Visual, Structured Cabling, ICT and Liquid Cooling.

As an end-to-end technology partner, SIBCA delivers consultancy, engineering, system integration, installation, commissioning and ongoing maintenance — working with international technology partners to provide customized, standards-compliant solutions.

The group also includes SIBCA Energy, a subsidiary active in renewable energy, oil & gas process technology and energy optimisation.

SIBCA Energy

Powering a more sustainable future

SIBCA Energy, a subsidiary of SIBCA Group chaired by Ibrahim Lari, is active in renewable energy, solar power, hydrogen vehicles, carbon capture, energy conservation, emissions reduction and Industry 4.0 automation.
